DEVREL TEMPLATE BIN STRUCTURE
=============================

Copy-paste these into the Media Pool. The numeric prefixes force sort order.

Master/
├── 01 — Footage/
│   ├── A-Roll (your face / talking head)
│   ├── B-Roll (cutaways, screen recordings, demos)
│   └── Slates (color charts, audio sync claps)
├── 02 — Audio/
│   ├── Dialogue (lavalier, shotgun mic)
│   └── Room tone (ambient bed, 30s minimum)
├── 03 — Music/
│   ├── Theme (your channel intro/outro music)
│   └── Beds (background tracks under VO)
├── 04 — Graphics/
│   ├── Logos (your channel/company brand)
│   ├── Lower thirds (saved Text+ presets)
│   ├── End cards (subscribe / next video)
│   └── Title cards (chapter dividers)
├── 05 — SFX/
│   ├── Transitions (whooshes, swooshes)
│   └── UI sounds (clicks, notifications)
├── 06 — VO/
│   ├── Scratch (rough VO for timing)
│   └── Final (re-recorded VO)
├── 07 — Timelines/
│   ├── MASTER_TIMELINE (your working timeline)
│   ├── YOUTUBE_VERSION (16:9 with Smart Reframe applied)
│   ├── LINKEDIN_VERSION (1:1 square)
│   └── SHORTS_VERSION (9:16 vertical)
└── 08 — Exports/
    ├── Drafts (intermediate renders for review)
    └── Final (final delivered MP4s)

Power Bins (project-library-scoped, not project-scoped):
├── BRAND ASSETS/
│   ├── Logo PNG/SVG
│   ├── Brand colors LUT
│   └── Standard end card
└── REUSABLE MUSIC/

Smart Bins (dynamic queries — set up after you have media):
├── Today's Footage (Date Modified = today)
├── Selects (Flag = Green)
└── Soundbites (Keywords contains "soundbite")

Tip: Keep this file printed/visible while creating bins for the first
time — it is faster than typing into the UI from memory.

Reference: Editor's Guide pp. 246-321 (Lesson 5, Project Organization)
